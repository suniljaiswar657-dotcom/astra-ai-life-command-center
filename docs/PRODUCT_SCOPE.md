# ASTRA — Initial Product Scope

## Product vision
A mobile-first AI Life Command Center that gives a user one organized place to plan, think, research, and manage everyday digital work.

## Intended outcomes
- See priorities and tasks in one place.
- Turn broad goals into manageable plans.
- Keep research and useful notes organized.
- Review progress without navigating disconnected tools.
- Use AI assistance where it adds practical value.

## Initial product areas

### Personal workspace
A clear home for active priorities, quick actions, and recent work.

### Tasks and planning
Create, organize, prioritize, and review tasks and plans.

### Research workspace
Capture questions, research notes, and useful findings with source context.

### Productivity overview
Show meaningful progress and upcoming work without overwhelming the user.

### Workflow command center
A future area for user-approved workflow automation. External actions require explicit authorization and a clear result.

## MVP boundary
The first MVP should prove one complete, useful user journey before expanding into multiple modules. The first journey will be selected after technical options are evaluated.

## Out of scope until separately approved
- Connecting ASTRA to another project or reusing another project's assets.
- Sending messages, publishing content, making purchases, or changing external accounts automatically.
- Claiming integrations or AI capabilities before they are implemented and tested.
- Paid infrastructure or paid API dependencies as a requirement for the initial MVP.

## Acceptance principles
- Usable on a phone-sized screen.
- Core interaction is understandable without a tutorial.
- Data handling is transparent.
- Errors and unavailable services are communicated honestly.
- Each milestone is tested before proceeding.
